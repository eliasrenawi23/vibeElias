Pick a logo concept

Two directions, both in taxi yellow and black, both with a Latin, Hebrew and Arabic lockup. Use the Concept switch at the top to try each one in the header and the app mock-up further down. Everything else on this page (colours, type, UI tone) is the same for either choice.

A. Road R Recommended

A rounded black tile with a yellow R. A dashed centre line runs up the stem and round the bowl, so the R is drawn as a road; the leg is the way out.

  • Reads at every size: the tile is the app icon as-is; favicon keeps the R, drops the road line.
  • Fits both businesses: says "motors", not only "taxi".
  • Black on yellow inverse for dark screens; one-colour version for stamps and forms.
  • Watch out: the road line needs about 48 px or more; below that use favicon.svg.

B. Roof sign

The yellow roof sign every Israeli taxi carries, on its roof line, with the R where the sign's lettering would be. On dark backgrounds the R is cut out and the roof turns white, like the taxi body.

  • Instantly says "taxi"; a distinctive silhouette on vehicles, signage and the office door.
  • Same R letterform as A, so the two can live together.
  • Watch out: the R is small inside the sign, so app icons need the black tile (build:icons --concept b) and the favicon is weak.
  • Watch out: yellow on white is low contrast (1.6:1); the black R and roof carry it. Leans to the taxi side more than the car dealership.

Suggestion: choose A as the logo for the whole business and keep B's roof sign as an illustration for the taxi side (licence pages, driver contracts, vehicle stickers).

Colour

Palette and contrast

White, taxi yellow and black: the colours of an Israeli taxi (white body, yellow roof sign, yellow plate with black digits). Green is kept for taxi licences, after the green plates that gave the "green number" its name. Ratios are WCAG 2.2, computed live from tokens.css; switch the theme to see the dark values.

Interface

Tone of the UI

Flat surfaces, hairline borders, one yellow action per view, black text on yellow. Plates, amounts and phone numbers are isolated left-to-right inside Hebrew and Arabic text. Flip the direction at the top: the layout mirrors, the numbers do not.
